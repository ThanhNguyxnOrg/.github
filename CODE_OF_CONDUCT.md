# 🌱 Code of Conduct

This organization values a respectful, constructive, and curiosity-driven environment.

Whether you're opening an issue, discussing ideas, or contributing code, please help keep interactions healthy and useful.

---

## ✅ Expected behavior

Please try to be:

- respectful
- patient
- constructive
- clear
- open to learning

---

## 💬 In discussions

Good discussion usually means:

- critique ideas, not people
- explain your reasoning
- keep things technical and useful
- assume good intent when possible

---

## 🚫 Unacceptable behavior

This includes:

- harassment
- insults
- hate speech
- personal attacks
- repeated bad-faith behavior
- intentionally disruptive communication

---

## 🛠️ Enforcement

As the maintainer of this personal organization, I reserve the right to moderate, remove, or ignore interactions that harm the space.

---

## ✨ Final note

This is a place for growth, experimentation, and real learning.  
Let's keep it respectful and worth building in.
